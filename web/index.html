DEFINITIONS

          

            Queueing model terminology
          

          Read the step-by-step guide →
        

        

          

            01 — Customer
            

              An entity processed by the system, such as a request, a job or a person.
            

          

          

            02 — Station
            

              A waiting queue and one or more servers that process its
              customers.
            

          

          

            03 — Service
            

              Processing time at a server; the interval between arrivals at a
              source.
